Impact

We pursue lasting change through community participation, environmental restoration and evidence-based action.

How We Understand Impact

REC measures and reflects on impact through multiple dimensions of change.

Community participation

Meaningful involvement of rural communities in identifying priorities, designing solutions and implementing activities.

Environmental restoration

Actions that protect and restore forests, ecosystems and natural resources that communities depend on.

Water security improvements

Strengthening access to and management of water resources in the face of climate and environmental pressures.

Climate adaptation

Supporting communities to understand risks and put in place practical measures that build resilience.

Youth engagement

Creating opportunities for young people to learn, lead and contribute to environmental and development solutions.

Knowledge sharing & partnerships

Research, evidence and collaborative relationships that improve the quality and reach of our work.

Impact Stories

Illustrative initiatives that demonstrate our approach. We share qualitative descriptions rather than unverified numbers.

Water Security

Santa Rural Water Security

Work aimed at strengthening rural water security and community resilience in Santa Village. The initiative focuses on practical improvements in water access and management, grounded in community participation and local knowledge.

Forest Restoration

Eco-Digital Forest Restoration Initiative

Focused on restoration of the Bafut-Ngemba Forest landscape. The initiative combines community participation with digital monitoring technologies, including the use of drones and environmental monitoring tools, to support climate action and forest recovery.

Partner to expand impact

Collaborate with REC to strengthen rural water security, restore forests and build climate resilience in Cameroon.
