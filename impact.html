Our Impact

Turning community knowledge, research and partnerships into practical action for people, communities and the environment.

How We Create Impact

REC focuses on practical and locally relevant solutions that strengthen communities, protect natural resources and support long-term resilience.

Community Participation

We involve communities in identifying priorities, sharing knowledge, designing solutions and taking part in implementation. Local participation is central to sustainable change.

Water Security

We promote responsible water management, protection of water sources and greater awareness of the importance of safe and sustainable water resources.

Forest Restoration

We support ecosystem protection and restoration while promoting community participation and the responsible management of forest resources.

Climate Resilience

Our work helps communities understand climate-related risks and identify practical approaches for adaptation and resilience.

Environmental Education

Through schools, community activities, seminars and awareness campaigns, we make environmental and climate knowledge more accessible and actionable.

Research & Knowledge

We connect research, local knowledge, field experience and evidence to better understand challenges and inform practical development interventions.

Impact in Practice

Our field initiatives demonstrate how knowledge, community participation and practical action can contribute to sustainable development.

Water Security

GBHS Bamendankwe Water Security Initiative

REC engaged students of Government Bilingual High School Bamendankwe to commemorate World Water Day through practical activities focused on water security and responsible water management.

The initiative combined educational lectures on water management with field activities, including the cleaning and protection of water sources within the school environment. The activity encouraged students to see water protection as a shared responsibility and to connect environmental knowledge with practical action.

Forest Restoration

Eco-Digital Forest Restoration Initiative

REC is advancing forest restoration and ecosystem protection through community engagement, environmental monitoring and appropriate digital technologies.

The initiative focuses on restoring degraded forest landscapes while strengthening community participation and improving the ability to monitor environmental change. Technology such as drones, sensors and digital tools can support field monitoring, planning and evidence-based restoration.

Community Action

Environmental Awareness & Community Engagement

REC uses community sensitization, environmental education, clean-up activities, seminars and knowledge-sharing events to encourage practical action on environmental and climate challenges.

These activities create opportunities for communities, students, institutions and partners to exchange knowledge and contribute to solutions that respond to local priorities.

Our Commitment to Meaningful Impact

We believe impact is more than counting activities. It is about whether knowledge leads to action, whether communities are strengthened and whether environmental improvements can be sustained over time.

Listen

We begin by listening to communities and understanding local priorities, experiences and knowledge.

Understand

We combine community knowledge with research, field observation and evidence to better understand environmental and development challenges.

Act

We translate knowledge into practical activities that respond to identified needs and opportunities.

Learn & Improve

We learn from implementation and partnerships so that future activities can become more relevant, effective and sustainable.
