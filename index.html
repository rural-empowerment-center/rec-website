Who We Are

Local action for sustainable communities.

Rural Empowerment Center (REC) is a non-profit organization established in 2016 and officially registered on June 28, 2021.

Based in Dschang, West Region, we work with communities, schools, institutions, researchers and partners to address environmental and development challenges.

Our approach connects community knowledge, research, education, environmental action and partnerships to create solutions that last.

What We Do

Practical action. Shared knowledge. Lasting impact.

REC brings communities and partners together around the issues that matter most for resilient rural development.

💧

Water Security

Supporting communities and institutions to improve water management, protect water sources and strengthen sustainable water resources.

🌱

Sustainable Agriculture

Promoting climate-smart and sustainable agricultural practices that strengthen food security, livelihoods and community resilience.

🌳

Forest Restoration

Supporting restoration and protection of forest ecosystems through community participation, technology, monitoring and environmental education.

🌍

Climate Action

Turning climate knowledge into practical local action through awareness, research, dialogue and adaptation.

♻️

Environmental Protection

Mobilizing communities and institutions around conservation, clean-up campaigns, ecosystem protection and responsible resource management.

🤝

Community Development

Building partnerships and strengthening local capacity so that communities can identify challenges, develop solutions and lead positive change.

Our Approach

Change that starts locally.

We connect community experience, science, education and partnerships to respond to complex environmental challenges.

Community-Led

Communities are partners, knowledge holders and leaders in the solutions we support.

Evidence-Based

We combine local knowledge with research, data and field experience to inform practical interventions.

Partnership Driven

We collaborate with institutions, schools, researchers, organizations and communities to expand local action.

Environment Focused

Protecting ecosystems and natural resources is central to building healthy and resilient communities.

Knowledge Sharing

Through webinars, campaigns, seminars and engagement, we help make knowledge accessible and useful.

Long-Term Resilience

Our goal is stronger communities that can continue creating solutions into the future.

Partnerships

Better together, stronger for communities.

REC welcomes collaboration with organizations, researchers, schools, public institutions, development partners, foundations and individuals who share a commitment to sustainable development.

Together, we can turn ideas, resources and knowledge into meaningful action for communities and the environment.
