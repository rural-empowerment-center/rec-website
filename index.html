Who We Are

Rural Empowerment Center (REC) is a youth-led, non-profit, community-based organization established in 2016 and formally registered in 2021. Based in Dschang (West Region) with a presence in Bamenda (North-West Region), we work alongside rural communities across Cameroon to promote practical solutions for resilient communities, environmental sustainability, climate adaptation, water security, sustainable agriculture and improved livelihoods.

What We Do

We focus on practical, community-centered action that strengthens resilience and protects the environment.

🌍

Climate Action

Supporting communities to understand climate risks and implement practical adaptation and mitigation measures grounded in local realities.

💧

Water Security

Improving water access, resource management and community resilience in the face of climate-related water challenges.

🌾

Sustainable Agriculture

Promoting climate-smart agriculture, sustainable land management and resilient rural livelihoods.

🌳

Environmental Protection

Supporting forest conservation, restoration, ecosystem protection and community stewardship of natural resources.

🤝

Community Development

Working with communities to design and implement inclusive solutions that improve livelihoods and strengthen local capacity.

📚

Youth & Environmental Education

Engaging young people through awareness campaigns, Environmental Clubs, seminars and community sensitization.

Our Approach

Everything we do is guided by three interconnected pillars.

1

Community

We place rural communities at the centre of every initiative, valuing local knowledge and ensuring meaningful participation.

2

Nature

We protect and restore ecosystems so that people and nature can thrive together for generations to come.

3

Action

We turn research and local insight into practical field work that delivers measurable, lasting change.
