What We Do

Our Work

Practical action for resilient communities, healthy ecosystems and sustainable development.

Our Areas of Action

Connecting community priorities with practical solutions

Rural Empowerment Center works across interconnected environmental and development challenges. Our programmes bring together communities, schools, institutions, researchers and partners to turn knowledge into meaningful action.

Our work is grounded in local realities while drawing on research, technology, education and partnerships to strengthen long-term resilience.

01

Climate Action

We support communities to understand climate risks, strengthen adaptive capacity and implement practical responses to changing environmental conditions.

Our work includes climate awareness, adaptation, community dialogue, knowledge sharing and support for approaches that reduce vulnerability.

02

Water Security

Water is central to health, livelihoods, agriculture and community resilience. REC supports activities that promote responsible water management and protection of water resources.

We engage communities and institutions on water conservation, water-source protection, awareness and practical approaches to improving water security.

03

Sustainable Agriculture

REC promotes agricultural approaches that contribute to food security, sustainable livelihoods and resilience to environmental and climatic pressures.

We support climate-smart agriculture, sustainable land management, knowledge exchange and approaches adapted to local farming conditions.

04

Forest Restoration & Conservation

Healthy forests and landscapes provide essential environmental services and support rural livelihoods.

REC supports forest restoration, ecosystem protection, environmental monitoring, community stewardship and awareness around the sustainable management of natural resources.

05

Environmental Protection

We work with communities and institutions to encourage responsible environmental management and strengthen awareness of the importance of healthy ecosystems.

Activities can include clean-up campaigns, environmental awareness, conservation activities, ecosystem protection and community-based environmental action.

06

Environmental Education & Awareness

Education is an important part of lasting environmental change. REC engages schools, communities and other stakeholders through sensitization activities, seminars, webinars, campaigns and knowledge-sharing initiatives.

We create opportunities for people to understand environmental challenges and participate in practical solutions.

07

Research & Knowledge

Research helps us understand environmental and development challenges and supports better decisions.

REC draws on field research, local knowledge, geospatial technologies, environmental data and community experience to generate and share useful evidence.

08

Community Development

Sustainable development requires communities to have the knowledge, opportunities and partnerships needed to shape their own future.

REC supports community participation, capacity strengthening, collaboration and practical initiatives that contribute to resilient livelihoods and improved community wellbeing.

09

Partnerships & Collaboration

Complex environmental and development challenges cannot be solved by one organization alone.

We work with communities, schools, researchers, institutions, civil society organizations and development partners to bring knowledge, resources and experience together around shared priorities.

How We Work

From knowledge to action

REC's work follows a practical cycle that connects community experience, evidence, action and learning.

01

Understand

We listen to communities, examine local realities and identify priorities and challenges.

02

Learn

We combine local knowledge, research, data and experience to better understand the issues.

03

Act

We support practical activities designed to respond to identified community and environmental priorities.

04

Learn & Share

We document experiences, evaluate lessons and share knowledge to improve future action and collaboration.
