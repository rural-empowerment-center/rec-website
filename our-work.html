Our Work

Practical, community-centered solutions for climate resilience, water security, sustainable agriculture and environmental protection.

Climate Action

REC supports communities to understand climate risks and implement practical adaptation and mitigation measures. We combine local knowledge with evidence-based approaches so that rural households and ecosystems can better withstand and respond to a changing climate.

Water Security

We work on water access, water-resource management, climate-related water challenges and community resilience. Our focus is on practical solutions that improve reliable access to water while protecting the sources that communities depend on.

Sustainable Agriculture

REC supports climate-smart agriculture, sustainable land management, improved agricultural practices and resilient rural livelihoods. We work with farmers and communities to strengthen food systems that are productive, environmentally sound and adapted to local conditions.

Environmental Protection

We support forest conservation, restoration, environmental education, ecosystem protection and community stewardship. Healthy ecosystems underpin rural livelihoods and long-term resilience.

Youth and Environmental Education

REC organizes awareness campaigns, Environmental Club activities, seminars, webinars and community sensitization. We engage young people as active agents of environmental stewardship and sustainable development.

Research and Knowledge

We use research, geospatial technologies, local knowledge and evidence to understand environmental and development challenges and to guide practical interventions. Knowledge generation and sharing strengthen both community action and partnership effectiveness.
