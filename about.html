About REC

Rooted in communities.
Driven by action.

Rural Empowerment Center works alongside communities and partners to create practical solutions for people, nature and future generations.

Our Story

Building solutions with communities

Rural Empowerment Center (REC) was established in 2016 and formally registered on 28 June 2021.

From the beginning, REC has focused on working alongside communities to respond to environmental and development challenges through practical action, knowledge sharing, research, education and partnerships.

Our work recognizes that issues such as climate change, water insecurity, environmental degradation, food insecurity and community development are interconnected. Sustainable solutions therefore require collaboration across communities, institutions, researchers and partners.

REC continues to develop initiatives that connect local knowledge with research and practical action, helping communities strengthen resilience and protect the natural resources on which livelihoods depend.

Our Vision

Resilient communities. Healthy environments. Sustainable futures.

We envision communities that are resilient, empowered and able to work together to build sustainable futures while protecting the ecosystems and natural resources that support life and livelihoods.

Our Mission

Turning knowledge into meaningful action

Our mission is to work with communities and partners to develop practical, inclusive and sustainable solutions that improve livelihoods, protect ecosystems, strengthen resilience and support community development.

Our Approach

Community knowledge meets research and action

REC believes that communities should be active participants in identifying challenges, developing solutions and implementing change.

We combine local knowledge, scientific research, education, technology and practical field experience to develop responses that are relevant to local realities.

Listen We begin by understanding community priorities, experiences and existing knowledge.
Learn We connect local knowledge with research, evidence and lessons from practice.
Act We support practical activities that respond to identified environmental and development needs.
Connect We bring communities, institutions and partners together around shared challenges.
Share We document experiences and share knowledge to encourage learning and replication.
Sustain We promote solutions designed to create lasting benefits for people and the environment.
Our Values

Principles that guide our work

Community Participation Communities are partners in identifying priorities, designing solutions and taking action.
Sustainability We seek solutions that create lasting benefits for people, communities and the environment.
Integrity We strive to be honest, transparent and responsible in our work and relationships.
Innovation We encourage practical ideas and appropriate technologies that respond to local realities.
Inclusion We value the participation and contribution of different groups and perspectives.
Partnership We believe collaboration can multiply knowledge, resources and impact.
Accountability We remain responsible to the communities we serve and to the partners who support our work.
Local Knowledge We respect community experience and recognize local knowledge as an important foundation for sustainable solutions.
Our Focus

Areas where we take action

Climate Action Supporting awareness, adaptation and practical responses to climate-related challenges.
Water Security Promoting responsible water management, water source protection and community awareness.
Sustainable Agriculture Supporting approaches that strengthen food security, livelihoods and resilience.
Environmental Protection Supporting conservation, environmental education, clean-up activities and ecosystem protection.
Forest Restoration Supporting restoration and protection of forest ecosystems and landscapes.
Community Development Supporting communities to identify challenges, build capacity and develop practical solutions.
Where We Work

Locally rooted, open to wider collaboration

REC has its head office in Dschang, West Region, with a branch presence in Bamenda, North-West Region.

Our activities engage communities, schools, institutions and other stakeholders around environmental, climate, water, agriculture and community development priorities.

While our experience and field engagement are rooted in local communities, REC is open to collaboration across Cameroon and with international organizations, researchers, institutions and development partners.
