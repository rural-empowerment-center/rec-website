About Rural Empowerment Center

A youth-led non-profit working with rural communities for resilient and sustainable development in Cameroon.

Our Story

Rural Empowerment Center (REC) was established in 2016 and formally registered on 28 June 2021. We are a youth-led, non-profit, community-based organization dedicated to strengthening rural communities through environmental sustainability, climate action, water security, sustainable agriculture, education and community-based development.

Our head office is in Dschang, West Region, Cameroon, with a branch presence in Bamenda, North-West Region. We work alongside rural communities to promote practical solutions that build resilience, protect ecosystems and improve livelihoods for people and future generations.

Our Vision

“Resilient rural communities living in harmony with nature and equipped to build a sustainable future.”

Our Mission

“To work with rural communities and partners to develop practical, inclusive and sustainable solutions that improve livelihoods, protect ecosystems and strengthen resilience to climate and environmental challenges.”

Where We Work

REC is rooted in Cameroon, with particular focus on the West and North-West Regions. From our bases in Dschang and Bamenda we engage rural communities on climate, water, agriculture and environmental priorities.

While our field presence is strongest in these regions, we are open to collaboration nationally across Cameroon and with international partners who share our commitment to practical, community-centered sustainable development.

Join us in growing change

We welcome partners, researchers, volunteers and supporters who share our vision of resilient rural communities.
