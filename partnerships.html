Partnerships for Greater Impact

We collaborate with communities, institutions, researchers, development organizations and other partners to turn shared knowledge and resources into practical action.

Working Together for Sustainable Change

Rural Empowerment Center believes that complex environmental and development challenges cannot be addressed by one organization alone.

Effective partnerships bring together community knowledge, research, technical expertise, institutional capacity, financial resources and practical experience.

We therefore seek partnerships that are respectful, transparent, mutually beneficial and focused on meaningful results for communities and the environment.

Who We Work With

Community Organizations

Working directly with community groups helps ensure that interventions respond to local priorities, knowledge and lived experiences.

Non-Governmental Organizations

We welcome collaboration with NGOs working in climate, environment, water, agriculture, livelihoods and community development.

Universities & Researchers

Research partnerships can strengthen evidence, innovation, knowledge generation and the application of research to real community challenges.

Government Institutions

We are open to collaboration with public institutions working to advance environmental protection, climate resilience, water security and sustainable development.

Donors & Foundations

Strategic funding partnerships can help transform community priorities and evidence-based ideas into sustainable field initiatives.

International Partners

We welcome international organizations and networks interested in knowledge exchange, technical cooperation, joint projects and community-centered development.

Why Partner With REC?

We bring together community engagement, environmental knowledge, research capacity and practical field experience.

Community Connection

Our work is grounded in engagement with rural communities and local institutions. We value local knowledge and community ownership.

Research & Evidence

We use research, field evidence, geospatial approaches and local knowledge to understand challenges and inform practical interventions.

Environmental Focus

Our thematic experience includes climate action, water security, sustainable agriculture, forest restoration and environmental protection.

Practical Implementation

We connect ideas and knowledge with activities on the ground, including community sensitization, school engagement, environmental action and field research.

Knowledge Exchange

Partnerships create opportunities for learning, dialogue, webinars, research collaboration and exchange of experiences across communities and institutions.

Long-Term Relationships

We seek partnerships built on trust, accountability, shared objectives and a commitment to sustainable results.

Building Connections Beyond Borders

REC values collaboration and knowledge exchange with organizations working toward shared development and environmental goals.

Our collaboration and knowledge-exchange relationships have included engagement with organizations such as Friends of Mzuzu Community (FOMC) in Malawi and CFPO in Nigeria.

These relationships demonstrate our openness to regional learning, professional exchange and joint approaches to community and environmental challenges.

We are also interested in developing new relationships with organizations, researchers and institutions that share our commitment to sustainable and community-centered development.

Ways We Can Collaborate

Partnership can take many forms depending on shared objectives, expertise and available resources.

Joint Projects

Co-design and implement community-based initiatives around climate, water, agriculture, forests and environmental protection.

Research Collaboration

Conduct research, surveys, knowledge exchange and evidence generation to better understand environmental and community development challenges.

Technical Support

Share technical expertise, training, tools and professional knowledge to strengthen local capacity.

Funding Partnerships

Support well-designed initiatives through grants, institutional support, project funding or other appropriate mechanisms.

Knowledge Exchange

Collaborate through webinars, seminars, publications, workshops and professional exchange.

Institutional Collaboration

Develop relationships between REC and schools, universities, public institutions, civil society organizations and development networks.

Let's Build Something Meaningful Together

If your organization, institution or network shares our commitment to resilient communities and a healthier environment, we would be pleased to explore opportunities for collaboration.
