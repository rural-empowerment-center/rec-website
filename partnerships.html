Partnerships for Greater Impact

Sustainable development requires collaboration between communities, civil society, governments, researchers, universities, donors and international partners.

Our Approach to Partnership

Rural Empowerment Center believes that lasting change is achieved when local knowledge, institutional capacity and external resources come together. We seek partnerships that are respectful, transparent and oriented toward practical results for rural communities and the environment.

We have experience of collaboration, partnership and knowledge exchange with organizations including Friends of Mzuzu Community (FOMC) in Malawi and Fundasaun Fitun Naroman Timor Leste (FINARTIL) in Timor-Leste. These relationships strengthen our ability to learn, share knowledge and pursue joint action across communities and countries.

Why Partner With REC?

Local Community Knowledge

Deep engagement with rural communities and respect for local experience, knowledge and priorities.

Cameroon-Based Field Presence

Grounded presence in the West and North-West Regions with capacity to work across rural Cameroon.

Youth Engagement

Strong focus on involving young people as learners, leaders and contributors to sustainable development.

Environmental Expertise

Practical experience in climate action, water security, forest restoration and sustainable agriculture.

Research Capacity

Ability to combine community-based research, geospatial tools and evidence to guide practical interventions.

Community-Centered Implementation

Commitment to inclusive, accountable and sustainable approaches that strengthen local ownership.

Start a Partnership

Whether you are a donor, NGO, research institution, government agency, university or community organization, we welcome dialogue about collaboration.
