Partner With REC

Together, we can strengthen rural communities, protect ecosystems and build practical solutions for a more resilient future.

Let's Work Together

Rural Empowerment Center welcomes partnerships with organizations and individuals committed to sustainable development and community-centered action.

Whether you are interested in supporting a field project, conducting research, sharing knowledge, providing technical expertise, mobilizing resources or developing a joint initiative, we would be pleased to explore opportunities for collaboration.

Ways to Partner With REC

Project Partnerships

Collaborate with REC to design and implement practical projects addressing climate change, water security, sustainable agriculture, environmental protection and community development.

Funding & Grants

Support community-centered initiatives through grants, project funding, sponsorship and other appropriate financial partnerships.

Research Collaboration

Work with REC on field research, community studies, geospatial analysis, knowledge generation and evidence-based approaches to sustainable development.

Technical Support

Share technical expertise, tools, training and professional knowledge that can strengthen the capacity of communities and local organizations.

Knowledge Exchange

Exchange experiences, research findings, local knowledge and good practices through workshops, seminars, webinars and learning activities.

Community & Field Support

Support environmental campaigns, water initiatives, restoration activities, education programmes and other community-based field actions.

Who Can Partner With Us?

We welcome collaboration with partners who share our commitment to resilient communities, healthy ecosystems and sustainable development.

Non-Governmental Organizations

NGOs and civil society organizations seeking local partners for community-based environmental and development initiatives.

Universities & Research Institutions

Academic and research institutions interested in field research, knowledge exchange, student engagement and applied research.

Government Institutions

Public institutions and local authorities interested in community engagement, environmental action and sustainable development initiatives.

Donors & Foundations

Funding organizations seeking locally grounded initiatives with practical community and environmental outcomes.

Companies & Private Sector

Businesses interested in responsible investment, environmental initiatives, community development and social impact.

Individuals & Volunteers

Professionals, researchers, students and volunteers who wish to contribute skills, knowledge, time or resources.

Priority Areas for Collaboration

Partnerships can support work across REC's key thematic areas.

Climate Action

Climate adaptation, resilience, mitigation and community awareness.

Water Security

Water access, water-resource management, conservation and climate-related water challenges.

Sustainable Agriculture

Climate-smart agriculture, sustainable land management and resilient food systems.

Forest Restoration

Forest conservation, restoration, ecosystem protection and community stewardship.

Environmental Education

School activities, environmental clubs, sensitization, campaigns and knowledge sharing.

Research & Innovation

Applied research, geospatial technologies, local knowledge and evidence-based development solutions.
