Field Action

From Knowledge to Action

Turning community knowledge, research and partnerships into practical action for people, water, agriculture and the environment.

Our Work in Practice

Action that begins with people and places

REC believes that meaningful change happens when knowledge is connected to action. Our field activities bring together communities, schools, institutions, researchers and partners around practical environmental and development priorities.

From protecting water sources and restoring forests to environmental education, community sensitization and climate action, our activities respond to real challenges and create opportunities for learning, participation and collaboration.

Featured Field Activities

Practical initiatives with communities

Our field experience continues to grow through community engagement, institutional collaboration and environmental action.

💧
Water Security

GBHS Bamendankwe Water Security Initiative

REC engaged students and the school community of Government Bilingual High School Bamendankwe around water security and responsible water management.

Activities included practical cleaning of water sources within the school environment and educational sessions on water management and the importance of protecting water resources.

🌳
Forest Restoration

Besali Forest Restoration

REC has undertaken environmental action focused on forest restoration and ecosystem protection in Besali.

The work emphasizes the importance of protecting forest ecosystems, restoring degraded landscapes and strengthening community understanding of the value of natural resources.

🌱
Education & Awareness

Environmental Education

REC works with schools and communities through sensitization activities, seminars, environmental education and knowledge-sharing initiatives.

These engagements encourage practical understanding of environmental challenges and support participation in local solutions.

Community Engagement

Building participation through action

Our field approach encourages communities and institutions to become active participants in identifying challenges and developing solutions.

01

Community Sensitization

We facilitate discussions and awareness activities on climate change, environmental protection, water, agriculture and sustainable resource management.

02

Clean-Up Campaigns

Community and institutional clean-up activities promote healthier environments and encourage shared responsibility for local spaces and natural resources.

03

School Engagement

We engage students and educational institutions through environmental clubs, seminars, lectures, practical activities and awareness campaigns.

04

Water Resource Protection

We promote awareness and practical action around protecting water sources and strengthening responsible water management within communities and institutions.

Knowledge & Research

Evidence supporting practical action

Field action is strengthened by research, local knowledge and continuous learning.

Research

Field Research

REC uses household surveys, interviews, focus group discussions, field observation and geospatial approaches to better understand environmental and development challenges.

Dialogue

Webinars & Knowledge Sharing

REC creates spaces for dialogue through webinars, seminars and other knowledge-sharing activities connecting researchers, practitioners, communities and partners.

Learning

Community Knowledge

We recognize local knowledge and community experience as important resources for understanding environmental change and developing locally appropriate responses.

Innovation

Technology & Evidence

Where appropriate, REC uses geospatial technologies, environmental data and other tools to support assessment, planning, monitoring and decision-making.

Climate & Livelihoods

Supporting resilient rural livelihoods

Climate change, environmental degradation, water insecurity and agricultural challenges are closely connected. REC therefore promotes approaches that consider these issues together.

Our work includes climate awareness, sustainable agriculture, environmental protection, water security and community capacity strengthening to help people respond to changing conditions.
