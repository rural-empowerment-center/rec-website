From Knowledge to Action

REC combines research, community knowledge and practical field action to strengthen rural resilience and protect the environment.

Practical Work on the Ground

Our field activities translate understanding into tangible community engagement and environmental action.

Community Sensitization

Environmental and climate awareness activities carried out with rural communities to build understanding of local risks and opportunities for collective action.

Clean-Up Campaigns

Community activities that promote cleaner and healthier environments while fostering a culture of shared responsibility for local spaces.

Environmental Clubs

Engagement with young people and schools through environmental education, building the next generation of stewards and change-makers.

Community Webinars

REC organizes regular knowledge-sharing activities, including webinars focused on climate change, sustainable agriculture, water security and environmental protection. These sessions create space for dialogue between communities, practitioners and partners.

Field Research

Community-based surveys, interviews, focus group discussions and geospatial analysis are used to understand local challenges. This evidence base helps design interventions that are relevant, realistic and owned by the communities they serve.
