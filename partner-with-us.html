Partner With Rural Empowerment Center

Whether you are a donor, NGO, research institution, government agency, university, company, foundation or community organization, we welcome opportunities to collaborate for sustainable development.

Ways to Partner

We offer flexible pathways for collaboration that match different strengths and priorities.

Fund a Project

Support community-based climate, water, agriculture and environmental initiatives. Your contribution helps turn local priorities into practical action on the ground.

Technical Partnership

Share expertise, technology, training and knowledge. Joint technical work strengthens the quality and sustainability of interventions.

Research Partnership

Collaborate on research, data collection, geospatial analysis and knowledge generation. Evidence helps communities and partners make better decisions.

Community Partnership

Work directly with communities to design and implement locally appropriate solutions. We value partners who respect community leadership and local knowledge.

Volunteer and Youth Engagement

Support environmental education and youth participation. Opportunities exist for volunteers, interns and youth-focused initiatives that align with our values.
